https://lifelike.build/ lifelike.build is a small village that runs itself, streamed live to your browser. Five families forage, farm, trade, gossip, raise children and dogs, and take their chances in the woods. Every judgment call a villager makes comes from Jev, TypeSafe's decision model, and a second small model keeps a running newspaper on what happens. Open any villager to see what they need, feel, remember, and why they did what they just did
