I made a thing called Prebrief that keeps my agents up to speed on what I’m doing across sessions. It pairs with Cartograph, my private knowledge and memory system; Engram is the public version of that. Unused memories fade, useful ones get reinforced, and past experiences consolidate into durable knowledge. Prebrief tells them what’s changed, and the memory layer gives them the context behind it, so I’m not constantly explaining everything again. I’ve had the private setup running since January across local models and API/CLI agents. Not sure how useful this is to anyone else, but it makes my Qwen 3.8-27B feel a lot closer to Terra on my builds lol.
