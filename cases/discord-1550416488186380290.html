Hey everyone 👋 I've been collecting public demos of what people are building with Jev, and I finally put them together into a directory: https://typesafeai.app To be clear up front: it's unofficial and independent — I'm not affiliated with TypeSafe. Every entry links back to the original repo or write-up, and lists the claimed metric, known limitations, and an evidence grade, so you can see exactly what's verifiable vs. author-reported. 13 cases on it right now, for example: 🎮 Playing Super Mario from raw emulator state 🛫 Searching Google Flights in ~7s (browser-use's jev-ultrafast) 🖥️ Driving macOS from OCR + accessibility state 🐝 Completing a StarCraft shareware mission 🚁 Choosing drone tactics from camera feed …plus code-review routing, supervising/gating coding agents, MCP integrations, Home Assistant automation, and more Two things I'd love from this community: What's missing?
