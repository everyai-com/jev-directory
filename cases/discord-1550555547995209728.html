Hi everyone, I just wrote up a small experiment testing TypeSafe’s Jev as a trusted and cheaper monitor alternate for AI Control. The interesting bit: a single yes/no question gets ~0.97 AUROC and catches ~90% of backdoors at a 2% audit budget, at a tiny fraction of the cost of an LLM monitor. But if the attacker can generate and score multiple backdoor variants, the picture changes pretty substantially.
