Hi TypeSafe Team, Early-access Jev evaluation results I've been running a preregistered synthetic evaluation of Jev across Choice, Noul and Score for potential typed advisory-decision use. Initial R1: 32 cases, 100% schema-valid. Choice 11/12 exact, Noul 9/10 at a 0.50 threshold with Brier 0.058, Score MAE 0.569.
